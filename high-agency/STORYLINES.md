# High Agency explainer — storylines

Source: https://www.highagency.com/ ("High Agency in 30 Minutes", George Mack).
The one idea: agency is a skill, not a trait. You can see it in how people handle a problem nobody has solved.
Facts used (all from the source): Wright brothers tested ~200 wing shapes, built a 180 lb engine, first flight 59 s / 582 ft, ~5.58B airline seats flown last year; the "tricycle" = clear thinking + bias to action + disagreeability; five low-agency traps (Vague, Midwit, Attachment, Rumination, Overwhelm).

## A. The 59 seconds (recommended)
- Device / form: the worked example (Wright brothers) as an object-to-object chain.
- What the viewer sees: one problem nobody had solved, worked through step by step: a wing becomes a wind-tunnel test, a test becomes an engine, the engine becomes the first flight. The flight's path then becomes today's sky full of planes.
- First 2 s: a stopwatch reading 0:59 over a hand-drawn flight path. Line: "59 seconds changed everything."
- Beats: hook (0:59) → the problem (no wing or engine that worked) → ~200 wing shapes tested → a 180 lb engine they built themselves → 582 ft → pull back to 5.58B seats → the tricycle named → "Agency is a skill. Build it."
- Look: blueprint-blue ground, white technical line drawings that draw on, one warm accent (signal orange) for the numbers.
- Sound: calm narration, a low pulsing score, pencil and wind-tunnel sound effects on the draws.

## B. The tricycle
- Device / form: the object is the explanation, in one frame that rebuilds.
- What the viewer sees: a tricycle drawn wheel by wheel (clear thinking, bias to action, disagreeability). Remove any one wheel and it tips over, so the frame rebuilds to show what each missing wheel looks like in real life.
- First 2 s: a three-wheeled bike rolls in; one wheel pops off and it crashes. "Agency needs all three."
- Beats: crash → wheel 1 → wheel 2 → wheel 3 → all three rolling → onto the high-agency spectrum → end line.
- Look: flat bold colour fields (one per wheel), thick geometric type, the bike as the carrier the eye follows.
- Sound: playful percussive music, a mechanical click on each wheel.

## C. Five traps (the bold one)
- Device / form: type carries the argument, cut on the beat, vertical 1080×1920.
- What the viewer sees: five low-agency traps, each its own typographic world (Vague: blurred letters snapping into focus; Rumination: a word looping in circles until it breaks out; Overwhelm: a wall of text collapsing to one line). Each trap is hit, then escaped.
- First 2 s: "You're stuck in one of these." The word "stuck" jams mid-animation.
- Beats: hook → trap 1 → … → trap 5 → "Agency is a skill." → highagency.com.
- Look: off-black and white, one acid-yellow accent, giant condensed type.
- Sound: hard-cut beat-driven track, no narration (or a fast voice).
