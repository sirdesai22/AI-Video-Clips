Landscape 16:9, 1920×1080, for YouTube, LinkedIn and web embeds. The length and scene count come from the video type below.
- Frame 0 is the thumbnail: the subject, a question or a number already on screen. Never black, a logo alone, a greeting or a title card with the topic's name.
- The hook lands in the first 2 s; the main idea is clear by scene 2.
- Something new on screen every 2–3 s. A framing may hold longer only while something acts inside it (a part draws on, a value counts, a cursor clicks).
- Safe areas: all content inside the 90% action-safe box (96 px in left and right, 54 px top and bottom); every word inside the 80% title-safe box (192 px left and right, 108 px top and bottom). Captions, when on, sit about 110–140 px from the bottom.
- Type floors: nothing meant to be read under 28 px; titles 4–9% of the frame height; a line holds 0.5 s plus 0.3 s a word. Too many words: cut words, never shrink type.
- `onScreen` at most 10 words, one line on screen at a time, no trailing period.
- One focal element per scene at display scale, filling at least 40% of the frame's height or width; never a small object in an empty frame.
- The last scene lands the one thing in one line and holds settled at least 2 s.
