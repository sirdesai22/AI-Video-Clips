Vertical 9:16 reel, 1080×1920, for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts. The length and scene count come from the video type below.
- Frame 0 is the thumbnail and the hook: the result, a face, a surprising number or one bold line already on screen. Never black, a logo, a greeting or the topic's name.
- The hook lands in the first second; the question or the change arrives in the first beat. Most viewers watch muted: the picture carries the hook without sound.
- A new beat every 2–3 s; shots of 1–2 s. One scene is one beat.
- Safe band: every word and the subject sit between 168 px and 1330 px from the top. Nothing in the top 168 px (status and search bar); below 1330 px only texture (caption, handle, buttons sit there); no text under the right-hand action rail. Write `visual` so it fits that band.
- Type floors: nothing meant to be read under 28 px; titles about 1.35× their landscape size (about 60–130 px); a claim line at most 5 words. Too many words: cut words, never shrink type.
- One subject, centred, stacked vertically. Never side-by-side panels or a wide chart: rebuild them as a vertical sequence.
- `onScreen` at most 6 words.
- When narrated, captions are burned in, so `spoken` must read well as captions: short phrases, no parentheses, no symbols the voice cannot say.
- The last scene pays off the hook and holds settled at least 2 s. Loop back to the first frame only when it fits the idea.
